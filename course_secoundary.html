Performance Booster – Class 8 - 10 (Maths & Science)

To help our students “Score More” in their exams, we have added several self-learning & assessment digital tools to supplement our face-to-face teaching offerings in two core subjects – Maths & Science. Digital enabled learning can be accessed through our Crest Academy web-based portal. It has 6 elements and serves as a strong Performance Booster when combined with classroom teaching.


Access to the Performance Booster requires a computer or mobile phone with an internet connection. The portal is 100% mobile friendly.


1. Recorded Lectures
– Each chapter is explained engagingly through short and focused videos to facilitate conceptual mastery through visual media. Each video covers a targeted sub-topic (7-8 minutes) and helps students preview or review school lessons.


2. E-Books
– Comprehensive study materials provided by Crest Academy are made available online for easy reference anytime. Students can make interactive notes and save them for revision.


3. Online Assignments
– At Crest Academy, students are provided with multiple assignments for each chapter, fully accessible online for practice and homework review.


4. Assessment MCQ
– Chapter-end quizzes test the conceptual understanding and application skills of students.


5. Diagnostic Unit Assessment (DUA) & Report
– A diagnostic tool helping each student identify strengths and weaknesses in each unit of Mathematics & Science, backed by actionable learning suggestions.


6. Online Revision & Doubt Clearing
– Live interactive doubt-clearing sessions twice a month led by senior faculty members. Sessions are recorded for anytime review.
