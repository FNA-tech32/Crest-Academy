IELTS & Language Proficiency Training

Preparing for IELTS with Crest Academy opens global academic and professional doors. Accepted by more than 10,000 organizations worldwide, our specialized English language program is meticulously crafted to build proficiency across all core communication modules: Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking.


Our program combines native-standard audio labs, extensive practice tests, real-time mock interviews, and personalized accent neutralization guidance to help each candidate achieve their desired band score (7.5+).


1. Recorded Video Lessons & Masterclasses
– Targeted lessons dissecting band scoring criteria, grammar essentials, and vocabulary expansion strategies.


2. E-Books & Cambridge Practice Sets
– Full access to authentic digital Cambridge IELTS material and practice sets curated by Crest Academy.


3. Online Mock Assessments
– Timed sectional and full-length computerized tests reflecting real testing conditions.


4. Speaking Clinics & 1-on-1 Interviews
– Personalized speaking sessions with senior evaluators followed by detailed rubrics and feedback.


5. Writing Evaluation & Feedback
– Line-by-line correction of Task 1 and Task 2 essays with score predictions and enhancement tips.


6. Doubt Clearing & Strategy Workshops
– Weekly live interactive doubt-clearing sessions focused on time-management and high-scoring techniques.
