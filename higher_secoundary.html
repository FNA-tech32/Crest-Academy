Performance Booster – Class XI - XII (Science & Commerce)

At Crest Academy, the Class XI and XII student undergoes a rigorous program based on foundational clarity and advanced problem solving. We believe in building a robust academic base in Class XI, empowering each student to excel in their Class XII Board Examinations and competitive entrance tests.


The program is structured to improve performance systematically in mid-term and final examinations through targeted revisions, mock tests, and personalized faculty mentorship.


1. Recorded Lectures
– In-depth concepts explained systematically through targeted chapter modules. Students can review topics anytime on web or mobile devices.


2. E-Books
– Curated study material and reference texts provided by Crest Academy are available 24/7 for quick reference, structured notes, and revision.


3. Online Assignments
– At Crest Academy, chapter-wise assignments, previous year question banks, and challenge problems are provided for extensive practice.


4. Assessment MCQ
– Periodic diagnostic tests simulate real board and entrance exam formats to track progress.


5. Diagnostic Unit Assessment (DUA) & Report
– Comprehensive analytics highlight each student's strong and weak chapters, guiding focused revision plans.


6. Online Revision & Doubt Clearing
– Live interactive doubt-clearing sessions twice a month led by senior faculty members with session archives for convenient recap.
